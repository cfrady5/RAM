What Sets RAM Apart

A Structured Pathway from Opportunity to Award

RAM connects government users and innovators through a transparent, legally grounded acquisition model designed to support discovery, assessment, visibility, and potential award.

Legally Grounded

Each marketplace is built around a government-issued announcement that establishes the basis for submission, review, evaluation, and potential follow-on procurement.

Built for Emerging Technology

RAM is especially useful for fast-moving and dual-use technology areas where government users may not know all available market options.

Designed for Measurable Outcomes

The model supports shorter acquisition timelines, increased competition, broader participation, and faster delivery of mission-relevant capability.
