Key Term

What does “awardable” mean?

Awardable status means a solution has successfully completed the required marketplace review process and is eligible for government discovery, engagement, and potential award using the applicable acquisition pathway.

Awardable does not automatically mean a company receives a contract. Contracting decisions remain based on mission need, funding, acquisition strategy, and government requirements.

Legal Foundation

Built on U.S. acquisition authority.

RAM marketplaces are grounded in government-issued announcements and applicable acquisition authorities. Many RAM efforts use Other Transaction Authority, or OTA, which allows the government to enter into flexible agreements for research, prototype, and production activities when statutory conditions are met.

In some cases, a successful prototype effort may support follow-on production without additional competition, consistent with the governing authority and the terms of the original solicitation or agreement.

This legal foundation is what makes RAM more than a discovery site. It creates a structured, competitive pathway for evaluation, marketplace visibility, government engagement, and potential award.
