For Government Users

How RAM works for government users.

For government users, RAM provides a structured pathway from mission need to discovery, review, engagement, and potential award.

  1. 01

    Define the Need

    Government teams identify a mission need, technical challenge, capability gap, or priority area.

  2. 02

    Issue an Announcement

    Each marketplace is built around a government-issued announcement that establishes the basis for submissions, evaluation, and potential follow-on procurement.

  3. 03

    Review Submissions

    Innovators submit concise solution materials that are reviewed by subject matter experts against published evaluation criteria.

  4. 04

    Identify Awardable Solutions

    Solutions that pass review are designated as awardable/award-ready and made available to government users within the marketplace.

  5. 05

    Discover, Engage, and Award

    Authorized government users can search marketplace solutions, engage companies, and move toward potential award using the applicable acquisition pathway.

What Government Users Gain

Discovery and engagement, with less friction.

Faster Discovery

Access a focused pool of solutions that have already been submitted and reviewed through a structured process.

Reduced Acquisition Friction

Shorten the path from market research to deeper engagement and potential award.

Portfolio-Level Visibility

Explore groups of capabilities across mission areas instead of relying only on one-off outreach.

Interoperability Across Marketplaces

Discover and leverage previously assessed technologies to reduce duplicative acquisition efforts and accelerate delivery to mission users.

For Innovators

How RAM works for innovators.

For innovators, RAM lowers barriers to entry by creating a clearer, more transparent way to present technologies to government users.

  1. 01

    Find the Right Opportunity

    Innovators identify a marketplace or topic area aligned to their solution.

  2. 02

    Submit a 5–7 Minute Pitch

    Companies provide a concise video-based solution pitch and supporting information.

  3. 03

    Receive Structured Review

    Subject matter experts review submissions against published evaluation criteria.

  4. 04

    Gain Marketplace Visibility

    Solutions that pass review become visible to authorized government users within the marketplace.

  5. 05

    Engage When There Is Mission Fit

    If a government user identifies a potential fit, the company may be contacted for further discussion, technical exchange, or potential procurement activity.

What Innovators Gain

A clearer, more accessible path to government.

Lower Barrier to Entry

A streamlined front-end process helps startups, small businesses, commercial firms, academic performers, and nontraditional companies participate.

Clearer Submission Format

The 5–7 minute video pitch helps industry explain the solution, team, use case, technical approach, and mission relevance.

Transparent Review

Published evaluation criteria help innovators understand how their solutions will be assessed.

Increased Discoverability

Awardable solutions can be found by authorized government users looking for relevant technologies.

Key Term

What does “awardable” mean?

Awardable status means a solution has successfully completed the required marketplace review process and is eligible for government discovery, engagement, and potential award using the applicable acquisition pathway.

Awardable does not automatically mean a company receives a contract. Contracting decisions remain based on mission need, funding, acquisition strategy, and government requirements.

Legal Foundation

Built on U.S. acquisition authority.

RAM marketplaces are grounded in government-issued announcements and applicable acquisition authorities. Many RAM efforts use Other Transaction Authority, or OTA, which allows the government to enter into flexible agreements for research, prototype, and production activities when statutory conditions are met.

In some cases, a successful prototype effort may support follow-on production without additional competition, consistent with the governing authority and the terms of the original solicitation or agreement.

This legal foundation is what makes RAM more than a discovery site. It creates a structured, competitive pathway for evaluation, marketplace visibility, government engagement, and potential award.
