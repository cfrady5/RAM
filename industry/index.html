The Innovator Pathway

How RAM works for innovators.

For innovators, RAM lowers barriers to entry by creating a clearer, more transparent way to present technologies to government users.

  1. 01

    Find the Right Opportunity

    Innovators identify a marketplace or topic area aligned to their solution.

  2. 02

    Submit a 5–7 Minute Pitch

    Companies provide a concise video-based solution pitch and supporting information.

  3. 03

    Receive Structured Review

    Subject matter experts review submissions against published evaluation criteria.

  4. 04

    Gain Marketplace Visibility

    Solutions that pass review become visible to authorized government users within the marketplace.

  5. 05

    Engage When There Is Mission Fit

    If a government user identifies a potential fit, the company may be contacted for further discussion, technical exchange, or potential procurement activity.

Submission FAQ

Common questions about submitting.

What is required for a submission?

Requirements vary by marketplace and opportunity. In general, be prepared to provide organization details, a capability description, technology area, target government use case, solution maturity, a 5–7 minute video pitch, and any supporting documentation required by the opportunity.

Who can submit?

Companies, startups, research teams, academic institutions, and nontraditional vendors seeking a clearer pathway into government acquisition.

How are submissions reviewed?

Submissions may be reviewed against published evaluation criteria with subject-matter expert input and mission-focused assessment.

Where do I find the requirements for a specific opportunity?

Submission requirements, evaluation criteria, and marketplace-specific guidance are published by each marketplace. Start with the marketplace links above and follow the current opportunity guidance.

Who should I contact with questions?

Contact the RAM team and select Innovator Submissions for help identifying the right resource or pathway.

Need help finding the right opportunity?

Connect with the RAM team for help identifying the marketplace or resource that best fits your capability.
